Your own pictures for the FBO lounge
====================================

Put PNG or JPEG pictures into this folder. They show with every livery.
The plugin builds them in the next time you enter the lounge (the first
time takes a moment while the screen is black); change a picture and it
is built in again.

The runner from the door to the reception: a name with "runner" in it.
  logo_runner.png     two panels (one in front of you at the start, one
                      before the door), up to 100 x 100 cm each
  logo_runner_1.png   one panel in the middle of the runner
  logo_runner_h.png   one panel in the middle, the picture turned along
                      the runner (it reads from the door towards the
                      reception), up to 100 x 260 cm
  The pictures read coming in through the door. A logo with a
  transparent background lies on the anthracite runner; a logo on a
  plain background (e.g. white letters on blue) colours the whole runner
  in that background colour, the logo on it as it is; any other picture
  fills the panel inside a bronze frame.

The screen behind the reception: a name with "tv" in it (e.g.
"logo_tv.png"). A picture about as wide as the screen (21:9) fills it;
any other lies in the middle on its own background colour (a
transparent logo on black, or on white if the logo is dark).

Music: MP3 files (OGG, FLAC and WAV work too) play in the lounge instead of
its own music, one after the other in name order and round again; the next
visit goes on with the next one. "fbo_music_volume" in GLF650_Walkaround.ini
sets the volume (0 = no music).

Only one picture of each kind is used: the first by name. WebP pictures
are not read: save them as PNG or JPEG. "fbo_custom = 0" in
GLF650_Walkaround.ini turns this off.
