FA8X Walkaround · Footstep Designer

Shape the three footstep sounds of the walkaround plugin, listen to them, and save FA8X_Walkaround_sounds.ini into the plugin folder. X-Plane picks the file up by itself within about two seconds, so you can keep walking and adjust as you go.

Less energy above 1.5 kHz sounds duller, a shorter ring-out drier (less echo). The six faint curves are the variants the plugin cycles through so no two steps sound the same.

Use it in X-Plane

  1. Save as FA8X_Walkaround_sounds.ini into
    X-Plane 12/Aircraft/Falcon 8X/plugins/FA8X_Walkaround/
    (the folder that also holds FA8X_Walkaround.ini).
  2. Within about two seconds the plugin rebuilds its steps and shows a short note. Or use Plugins › FA8X Walkaround › Reload footstep sounds.
  3. Delete the file to go back to the built-in sounds.

How a step is built

Impact – the heel strike: filtered noise. A low cut-off and more filter stages make it duller, a short attack makes it hard, a rounded attack soft.

Body – an optional deeper thud underneath. The hollow resonance adds the boomy, metallic character of the airstair.

Grit – sparse grains of sand and small stones crunching under the sole; this is what makes asphalt sound like asphalt. Keep it low, or the step gets bright and hissy.

Ball of the foot – the same once more, a few milliseconds later and quieter, like a real heel-to-toe step. Set its level to 0 for a single impact.

Overall volume in X-Plane is footstep_volume in FA8X_Walkaround.ini; X-Plane's interior sound volume applies too. Chrome and Edge save straight into the file; other browsers download it, then move it into the plugin folder.
