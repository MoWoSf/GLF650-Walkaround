FA8X Walkaround - credits for the 3D models and sounds
=======================================================

The vehicles and the people in them are based on these models, all licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).


Luggage van
-----------
"2020 Mercedes-Benz EQV" (https://skfb.ly/pFqUL) by tonielpro520 is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made for this add-on:
- converted to an X-Plane object (OBJ8), welded and reduced in detail
  (431,000 to 131,000 triangles), scaled to the real length of 5.14 m
- repainted Obsidian black; the flat colours given a gloss and metalness per
  material, everything packed into one texture with a normal and a light map
- the wheels, the front wheels' steering, the brake calipers and the tailgate
  (with its glass, trim and third brake light) made movable
- the lights made to light up: daytime running lights, low beam, tail and brake
  lights, third brake light, reverse lights
- the Philippine number plate replaced by a German one ("M FA 8000")
- the third row of seats and the parcel shelf taken out; the load space lined
  with a floor, side walls up to the windows, rounded wheel arches and a face
  under the second row, all black
- the whole van drawn from both sides, so it is not see-through anywhere


Passenger car
-------------
"Mersedes- Benz Maybach GLS 600" (https://skfb.ly/oRoZT) by Black Snow is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made for this add-on:
- converted to an X-Plane object (OBJ8), scaled to the real length of 5.21 m
  and reduced in detail
- the running gear, engine and brakes of the BeamNG model left out; new brake
  discs and calipers
- coloured per material and part (the model has no textures): its own white
  paint, chrome, a saddle brown cabin, privacy glass behind the B-pillar
- the rear doors (with their trim and windows), the wheels, the front wheels'
  steering and the lights made movable or able to light up
- number plates added ("M FA 600")
- the chauffeur and two passengers put inside (see below)
- the window panes left out while you ride in the back yourself (from the FBO)


Chauffeur and passengers
------------------------
"Business Man - Low Polygon game character" (https://skfb.ly/6Ezvq) by manoeldarochadeoliveira is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made for this add-on:
- posed sitting in the car from his "sitting_idle" pose: the legs reach into
  the footwell, the chauffeur leans forward and holds the steering wheel
- three copies merged into the car's object (the chauffeur, two passengers in
  the rear seats who are hidden once they have got out) and their texture
  packed into the car's texture

Other parts
-----------
- The bags, the door mat, the ground service phone and the FBO rooms (walls,
  frosted glass facade, doors, lights, signs, the briefing room's furniture,
  clocks and notice board, the departure board and weather screens) are
  original work for this add-on.
- The signs' lettering and pictograms are drawn with the fonts Inter and
  Noto Sans Symbols 2 / Noto Emoji (SIL Open Font License 1.1,
  https://openfontlicense.org), as shipped with Blender.
- The FBO lounge's music was composed for this add-on (tools/mkmusic.c).
- The passengers' voices were generated offline with Kokoro v1.0 (hexgrad,
  Apache License 2.0, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) through
  sherpa-onnx (Apache License 2.0, https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx),
  tools/gse/mkvoices.ps1.
- The footstep sounds are synthesised by the plugin itself.
